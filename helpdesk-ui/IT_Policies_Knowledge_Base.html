VPN & Remote Access Guide

SLA & Expectations: VPN access is granted by default to all employees. Connection takes <1 minute. If escalated to a ticket, IT response time is within 4 hours.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Navigate to the IT Portal at support.acmecorp.com/vpn.
  2. Download the Acme Corp VPN Client (GlobalProtect) for your operating system (Windows or macOS).
  3. Run the installer and follow the on-screen prompts to complete installation.
  4. Launch the VPN Client from your system tray or applications folder.
  5. In the Portal Address field, enter: vpn.acmecorp.com.
  6. Click Connect and enter your standard Acme Corp email and password.
  7. Approve the Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) prompt sent to your mobile device.

Troubleshooting / Common Errors

Problem: You do not receive the MFA prompt on your phone.

Solution: Open your authenticator app and manually type the 6-digit code into the VPN prompt window instead of waiting for the push notification.


Problem: VPN connection hangs at "Connecting..." or shows Error 800.

Solution: Disconnect from your home Wi-Fi, reconnect to it, and restart your computer. This clears local DNS caching issues 90% of the time.

Escalation Rule: If you have restarted your computer and still cannot connect after 3 attempts, or if your MFA device is lost/broken, submit an IT Ticket with the category "Network".

Password Reset & Account Security Policy

SLA & Expectations: Self-service resets are instantaneous. If IT assistance is required, expect a resolution within 2 hours.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Go to support.acmecorp.com/password on any device (including your personal smartphone).
  2. Enter your Acme Corp email address and click "Forgot Password".
  3. Verify your identity using your backup MFA method (SMS text or Authenticator app).
  4. Create a new password. The password MUST meet these complexity rules:
    • Minimum of 12 characters.
    • Contain at least one uppercase letter and one lowercase letter.
    • Contain at least one number.
    • Contain at least one special symbol (e.g., !, @, #, $, %).
  5. Click "Save". You must now use this new password across all Acme Corp systems immediately.

Troubleshooting / Common Errors

Problem: "Account is temporarily locked" error message.

Solution: Your account locks automatically after 5 failed login attempts. You must wait 15 minutes for the system to automatically unlock before trying again.


Problem: New password is rejected during creation.

Solution: Ensure you are not reusing any of your last 5 passwords, and double-check that your new password does not contain your first name, last name, or the word "Acme".

Escalation Rule: If you are locked out of your MFA device and cannot perform a self-service reset, submit an IT Ticket with the category "Security" or call the IT hotline directly.

Security Incident Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

SLA & Expectations: Security incidents are Priority: Critical. The IT Security team will respond within 15 minutes, 24/7.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Identify the Threat: Did you click a suspicious link? Enter your password on a weird site? Lose your company phone or laptop?

Printer & Office Network Setup

SLA & Expectations: Connecting to Wi-Fi and mapping printers is immediate via self-service. If an IT dispatch is required for broken hardware, response time is 8 hours.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. To Connect to Office Wi-Fi:
    • Select the network named AcmeCorp-Secure.
    • Enter your standard company email and password.
    • Accept the security certificate if prompted.
  2. To Map an Office Printer:
    • Ensure you are connected to AcmeCorp-Secure or plugged into an office ethernet port.
    • Open the Start Menu (Windows) or System Settings (Mac) and go to "Printers & Scanners".
    • Click "Add a Printer".
    • Search for the printer ID printed on the physical sticker on the machine (e.g., NY-Floor3-Color).
    • Click "Add". Drivers will install automatically.

Troubleshooting / Common Errors

Problem: The printer is added to your computer, but nothing prints.

Solution: Walk to the physical printer. It likely has a paper jam or is out of paper. The LCD screen on the printer will tell you how to resolve this.


Problem: Cannot connect to AcmeCorp-Secure Wi-Fi; password is rejected.

Solution: If you recently changed your Acme Corp password, your computer may be trying to use the old one. Choose "Forget this Network" in your Wi-Fi settings, then reconnect and type the new password.

Escalation Rule: If the printer has a mechanical failure (e.g., grinding noise, broken screen), or if you are completely unable to connect to the secure Wi-Fi after forgetting the network, submit an IT Ticket with the category "Hardware".
