💻 Hardware Request Policy

⏱ SLA & Expectations: Manager approval takes up to 24 hours. Once approved, standard hardware delivery takes 3–5 business days. Critical replacements for broken primary devices take 1–2 business days.

How to Request New Hardware

  1. Log into the IT Portal at support.acmecorp.com.
  2. Click on "Hardware Catalog".
  3. Select the equipment you need from the catalog.
  4. In the justification box, briefly explain the business need (e.g., "Required for new project X" or "Current mouse is unresponsive").
  5. Submit the request.
  6. Your direct manager will receive an automated email to approve the expense (for items requiring approval).
  7. Once approved, IT will provision the equipment and email you tracking or pickup details.
⚠️ Broken/Damaged Primary Device (Emergency): If your primary work laptop is physically broken, water-damaged, or stolen — do NOT use the standard hardware catalog. Instead:
  1. File a "Critical" priority IT ticket immediately.
  2. Bring the damaged device to the nearest IT desk (if possible).
  3. IT will issue an emergency loaner laptop within 1–2 business days.

Returning Old Equipment

  1. When you receive replacement hardware, you must return the old device within 5 business days.
  2. Back up any personal files (they will be wiped).
  3. Bring the old device to the IT desk or schedule a pickup via support.acmecorp.com/returns.
  4. IT will securely wipe the device, update the asset tracking system, and issue you a return receipt.
ℹ️ Asset Tags: All company hardware has a silver asset tag sticker (e.g., ACME-L-04521). Do not remove this sticker. If it falls off, report it to IT so they can re-tag the device. You will need this number when filing hardware tickets.

Troubleshooting / Common Errors

Problem: You haven't received the equipment after a week.

Solution: Check the portal to see the status. The most common delay is that your manager has not clicked the "Approve" button in their email yet. Please remind them.


Problem: You received the wrong equipment or a defective item.

Solution: Do not open or use the incorrect item. File a new ticket referencing your original request number and IT will arrange a swap within 2 business days.

🚨 Escalation Rule: If your equipment arrives damaged, if your request has been approved but stuck in "Processing" for more than 4 business days, or if you need to report a lost/stolen company device, submit an IT Ticket immediately.
