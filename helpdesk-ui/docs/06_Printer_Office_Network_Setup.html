How to Connect to Office Wi-Fi (Employees)

  1. Open your device's Wi-Fi settings.
  2. Select the network named AcmeCorp-Secure.
  3. Enter your standard Acme Corp email and password.
  4. Accept the security certificate if prompted.
  5. You should be connected within a few seconds. A lock icon will appear confirming a secure connection.
ℹ️ Guest Wi-Fi: If you have a visitor or contractor who needs internet access, direct them to the front desk/reception. They will receive a daily password for AcmeCorp-Guest. Guest Wi-Fi provides internet access only — it cannot access internal company systems, printers, or file shares.

How to Map an Office Printer

  1. Ensure you are connected to AcmeCorp-Secure Wi-Fi or plugged into an office Ethernet port.
  2. Windows: Open Start Menu → Settings → Bluetooth & Devices → Printers & Scanners → Click "Add Device".
  3. macOS: Open System Settings → Printers & Scanners → Click the "+" button.
  4. Search for the printer ID printed on the physical sticker on the machine (e.g., NY-Floor3-Color or BLR-Floor2-BW).
  5. Select it and click "Add". Drivers will install automatically.
  6. Print a test page to confirm the connection.

How to Scan a Document to Email

  1. Place your document face-down on the printer's flatbed scanner, or in the top document feeder.
  2. On the printer's touch screen, tap "Scan" → "Scan to Email".
  3. Enter your Acme Corp email address as the recipient.
  4. Choose your preferred format: PDF (recommended) or JPEG.
  5. Tap "Start Scan".
  6. The scanned document will arrive in your email inbox within 1–2 minutes.
⚠️ Printing Confidential Documents: If you are printing sensitive information (HR records, financials, legal contracts), use the "Secure Print" feature. When printing, select "Secure Print" in the print dialog and set a 4-digit PIN. You will need to enter this PIN on the printer's touch screen before it releases the print job. This prevents confidential pages from being left unattended in the tray.

Troubleshooting / Common Errors

Problem: The printer is added to your computer, but nothing prints.

Solution: Walk to the physical printer. It likely has a paper jam or is out of paper/toner. The LCD screen on the printer will display a message telling you exactly how to resolve this (e.g., "Open Tray 2 and clear paper"). If the screen says "Toner Low", contact the office admin — do not attempt to replace toner yourself.


Problem: Cannot connect to AcmeCorp-Secure Wi-Fi; password is rejected.

Solution: If you recently changed your Acme Corp password, your computer may be trying to use the old one. Choose "Forget this Network" in your Wi-Fi settings, then reconnect and type the new password.


Problem: "Scan to Email" is not working — scanned documents never arrive.

Solution: Double-check that you typed your email address correctly on the printer screen (typos are the most common cause). If the email is correct, check your Spam/Junk folder. Scanned emails come from printer@acmecorp.com which spam filters may flag the first time.

🚨 Escalation Rule: If the printer has a mechanical failure (e.g., grinding noise, broken screen, visible damage, repeated paper jams that won't clear), or if you are completely unable to connect to the secure Wi-Fi after forgetting the network and retrying, submit an IT Ticket with the category "Hardware". Include the printer name (from the sticker) in your ticket.
