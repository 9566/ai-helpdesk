🔐 Password Reset & Account Security Policy

⏱ SLA & Expectations: Self-service resets are instantaneous. If IT assistance is required (e.g., MFA device lost), expect a resolution within 2 hours.
📜 Password Policy Summary:
  • Expiry: Passwords expire every 90 days. You will receive email reminders at 14 days, 7 days, and 1 day before expiry.
  • History: You cannot reuse your last 5 passwords.
  • Lockout: After 5 failed attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes automatically.
  • Complexity: Minimum 12 characters, must include uppercase + lowercase + number + symbol.
  • Prohibited: Password must not contain your first name, last name, email username, or the word "Acme".

How to Reset Your Password (Self-Service)

  1. Go to support.acmecorp.com/password on any device (including your personal smartphone).
  2. Enter your Acme Corp email address and click "Forgot Password".
  3. Verify your identity using your backup MFA method (SMS text or Authenticator app).
  4. Create a new password meeting the complexity rules listed above.
  5. Click "Save". Your new password takes effect within 5 minutes across all Acme Corp systems (email, VPN, intranet, Wi-Fi).

How to Change Your Password (Before Expiry)

  1. Press Ctrl + Alt + Delete on your Windows computer and select "Change a password".
  2. On macOS: Open System Settings → Users & Groups → Change Password.
  3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
  4. Click "OK". If connected to VPN, reconnect with the new password.

How to Set Up MFA (First Time)

  1. Visit support.acmecorp.com/mfa-setup and log in with your Acme Corp email.
  2. Download the Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator app on your phone.
  3. Scan the QR code shown on screen with your authenticator app.
  4. Enter the 6-digit verification code from the app to confirm enrollment.
  5. Optionally, add a backup phone number for SMS-based MFA as a secondary method.
ℹ️ Important: If you get a new phone, you must re-enroll MFA before wiping or trading in your old phone. Visit support.acmecorp.com/mfa-setup while you still have access to your old device to transfer MFA to the new one.

Troubleshooting / Common Errors

Problem: "Account is temporarily locked" error message.

Solution: Your account locks automatically after 5 failed login attempts. You must wait 15 minutes for the system to automatically unlock before trying again. Do not call IT unless the lockout persists after 15 minutes.


Problem: New password is rejected during creation.

Solution: Ensure you are not reusing any of your last 5 passwords, and double-check that your new password does not contain your first name, last name, or the word "Acme". The password must be at least 12 characters.


Problem: Password was changed but VPN / Wi-Fi still asks for the old one.

Solution: After changing your password, disconnect and reconnect to VPN and Wi-Fi using the new password. On Wi-Fi, choose "Forget this Network" first, then reconnect.

🚨 Escalation Rule: If you are locked out of your MFA device (lost/broken phone) and cannot perform a self-service reset, submit an IT Ticket with the category "Security" or call the IT hotline directly. You will need to verify your identity in person with a government-issued photo ID before IT can reset your MFA.
