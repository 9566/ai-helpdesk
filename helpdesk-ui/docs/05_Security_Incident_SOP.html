🛡️ Security Incident Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

⚠️ TIME IS CRITICAL. If you suspect a security incident, follow these steps IMMEDIATELY. Do not wait.
⏱ SLA & Expectations: Security incidents are Priority: CRITICAL. The IT Security team will respond within 15 minutes, 24/7.

What Counts as a Security Incident?

Report immediately if ANY of the following occur:

  • 🔗 You clicked a suspicious link in an email, text, or chat message.
  • 🔑 You entered your password on a website you now believe is fake.
  • 📱 You lost or had stolen a company laptop, phone, or USB drive.
  • 👤 You notice unauthorized access to your account (emails you didn't send, files you didn't open).
  • 💾 You plugged in an unknown USB drive or external device found in the office.
  • 📞 Someone called you claiming to be IT and asked for your password or remote access.
  • 🔒 Your files are suddenly encrypted or renamed and you see a ransom note (ransomware).
  • 📧 You accidentally sent sensitive data (customer records, financials) to the wrong person.

Step-by-Step Response Instructions

  1. STOP: Do not try to fix it yourself. Do not click any more links. Do not delete the suspicious email.
  2. DISCONNECT (if applicable): If you suspect malware or clicked a malicious link:
    • Disconnect from Wi-Fi immediately.
    • Unplug the Ethernet cable if connected.
    • Do NOT turn off the computer (forensic evidence may be lost).
  3. REPORT: Go to support.acmecorp.com (from another device if yours is compromised) and submit a CRITICAL priority ticket under the category "Security".
  4. DETAIL THE INCIDENT: In your ticket, provide:
    • What happened (e.g., "I clicked a link in a fake invoice email").
    • The exact time it occurred.
    • Which device you were using (include asset tag number if possible).
    • Whether you entered any passwords or personal information.
    • Attach a screenshot of the suspicious email/page if possible.
  5. CHANGE YOUR PASSWORD: If you entered your credentials on a suspicious site, change your Acme Corp password immediately from a different, clean device at support.acmecorp.com/password.
  6. WAIT: Leave the affected device powered on but disconnected. The Security team will contact you via phone call or in person within 15 minutes.

Troubleshooting / Common Scenarios

Scenario: You received a suspicious email but did NOT click anything.

Action: This is NOT a critical incident. Simply use the "Report Phishing" button built into your Outlook/Gmail client. The security team will investigate and block the sender domain. Do not forward the email to colleagues.


Scenario: You lost your company phone while traveling.

Action: File the Critical ticket immediately so IT can remotely wipe the device. Do not wait until you return. Every hour of delay increases the risk of data exposure. IT can wipe the phone remotely even if it is turned off (it will wipe on next power-on).


Scenario: A colleague told you they were scammed — should you report it?

Action: Yes. Encourage them to file their own ticket, but also file one yourself referencing their name. Security incidents can spread laterally — if one account is compromised, related accounts may also be targeted.

Post-Incident Follow-Up

ℹ️ What happens after you report:
  1. The Security team will investigate and contain the threat.
  2. If your device was compromised, IT may need to wipe and re-image it (you will receive a loaner laptop during this time).
  3. You may be asked to complete a brief Security Awareness Refresher (a 10-minute online training module).
  4. The incident will be documented in the company's security log (this is not punitive — it helps IT improve defenses).
  5. IT will send you a summary email confirming the incident is resolved and what steps were taken.
🚨 Escalation Rule: If you are unable to access the IT Portal because your account is compromised, you must immediately call the 24/7 Security Hotline at 1-800-555-ACME. Do not wait. Do not email. Call directly. If you suspect a data breach involving customer data, also notify your manager and the Data Protection Officer at privacy@acmecorp.com.
